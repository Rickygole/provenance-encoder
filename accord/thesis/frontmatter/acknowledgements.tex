  I thank my thesis chair, Dr.\ Jamell Dacon, of the Department of Computer
  Science, for supervision that improved this work most where it was weakest.
  Several of the results reported here exist because he asked what the control
  was, and the standard this thesis tries to meet, that a claim be checked
  before it is made, is one I learned from working with him.

  I thank the board-certified pharmacist who produced the gold standard. That
  annotation is the scarcest artifact in this project. It cannot be simulated
  and it cannot be scaled, and every evaluation in Chapter~\ref{ch:results}
  rests on it. I thank the second coder who independently re-coded nineteen of
  the seventy-five surveyed papers without sight of the primary coding; the
  reliability figures reported for the survey exist only because that work was
  done blind.

  I thank the members of my thesis committee for reading a document whose
  central result is a negative one, and the Department of Computer Science at
  Morgan State University for the resources and the time this work required.
