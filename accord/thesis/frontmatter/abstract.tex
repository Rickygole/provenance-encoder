  A contradiction between a nursing note and a discharge summary can carry out of the
hospital a medication instruction the care team had already stopped.
This thesis builds a standard four stage pipeline for detecting them in an
intensive care database and runs, at each stage, the control that separates real
failure from artifact. The pipeline does not work. The weak annotator flags 261
of 299 gold pairs against 12 confirmed, fifteen to twenty-four times the
confirmed rate at eight billion parameters against at most three and a half
times on public text with the same models, so over-calling transfers across
domains and its magnitude does not. That marginal alone fixes an agreement
ceiling before any label reaches training, and on a real pool of nine labeller
configurations measured this way, five could not have cleared the conventional
significance threshold at any outcome their marginal permitted. Three credited
architectural components are audited in the code behind their numbers: one
parameter falsified as an initialisation artifact, one metric vacated by label
leakage, one module never trained. The nulls this pipeline returns are
informative above a minimum effect the evaluation's own class counts fix, and a
pre-registered positive control on a public benchmark shows the training
apparatus resolves a real difference where one exists. The work does not show
the task unsolvable. It contributes a way of diagnosing a failed pipeline and
the recommendation that a one-prompt baseline be beaten first.
