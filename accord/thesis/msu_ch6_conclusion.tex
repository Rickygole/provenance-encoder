\chapter{Summary and Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{What Was Asked}
\label{sec:conc-asked}

A nursing note records that a loop diuretic was held because the patient had
become hypovolemic. The discharge summary written five days later instructs that
the same drug be continued at the admission dose. Only one of those documents
leaves the hospital with the patient, and it is not the one containing the
reason the instruction is wrong.

This thesis asked whether that disagreement can be detected automatically from
paired clinical notes in MIMIC-III \citep{johnson2016mimic}, and built a
standard-shaped pipeline to
answer it: rule-based candidate mining, language model weak supervision, and a
purpose-built neural detector over a domain-pretrained clinical encoder. Five
questions were posed, four about the stages and one about the field that
publishes pipelines like this one. Every stage failed a control, and not every
failure carries the same weight, a distinction Section~\ref{sec:conc-found}
keeps rather than collapses.

\section{What Was Found}
\label{sec:conc-found}

Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion}, Table~\ref{tab:disc-spine} synthesises what each
pipeline stage reported before its control and what the control changed; this
section states the outcome once rather than walking each stage again. Every
one of the five questions failed a control. The candidate pool's enrichment is
real but its precision is bounded above by an exemplar defect that made the
evidence displayed to the annotator, the weak labeller, and the detector
independent of the evidence that triggered the candidate. The weak labeller's
marginal fixes an agreement ceiling before training begins, and the labeller
that actually supplied the detector's training signal could not have cleared
the conventional significance threshold at any outcome its own marginal
permitted, which is a precondition rather than a coincidence. None of three
credited architectural components is left holding what it was credited with,
and the three failures are of three different kinds. The trained detector's
ranking is at chance under both evaluation bases and under subject-disjoint
splits, a result the labeller's marginal already predicts rather than one
that needed explaining after the fact, and a TF-IDF logistic regression ranks
above both neural configurations, which is either surface-form signal or
small-sample noise and is not established either way. A $75$-paper corpus
deliberately enriched toward careful work still finds the one control this
survey measures rare, auditing the selection step under a strict reading in
only a small minority of it. Not every failure carries the same weight, a
distinction Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion} keeps rather than collapses.

\section{What the Controls Changed}
\label{sec:conc-controls}

Without controls, everything above reads as a small underpowered study that
found nothing. The controls convert the same results into measurements with a
stated resolution.

The positive control carries the most weight. The device adapts one
observational epidemiology formalised decades ago
\citep{lipsitch2010negative}: run the analysis where the answer is known, and
read the result as a statement about the instrument. Run on AG News with the
identical training harness and an adjudication rule committed before any run,
the resolved pretraining benefit is $4.53$ accuracy points at convergence; the
same harness, on the in-hospital mortality task built for this control,
returns a null, $+0.0031$. What that pairing licenses, and does not, for the
contradiction evaluation's own sensitivity is argued in
Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion}, Section~\ref{sec:disc-null}, not repeated here.
The general proposition that a pretraining objective predicts downstream
utility poorly is not established here; it is established elsewhere and at a
scale this project cannot approach \citep{lourie2025scaling,feng2026tessera}.
The sweep is a control, not a contribution.

The decontamination control changed the headline. Excluding the evaluation
identifiers at load time moved AUROC on the same $253$ pairs from $0.4595$ to an
ensemble $0.5145$, a move of $0.0550$ that Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion} reports as
untested and that lands nowhere useful in either direction. The value of the
control is not that move. It is that the number reported before it was
contaminated, and nothing in the metrics said so.

The sign provenance control changed no value and several conclusions.
Retrofitting signed storage for quantities persisted through an absolute value
reproduced every prior figure exactly, maximum discrepancy $0.0$, while
reversing seven of the fourteen comparable drift slopes. The demographic parity
slope is $-0.0078$ signed under one selection regime and $+0.0118$ under the
other, and both invert when stored as magnitudes. A quantity stored as a
magnitude licenses no directional claim.

The randomisation controls were run on the second system and not on the
clinical detector, and the arm built to test the credited component did not
settle anything: the pre-specified role-label control ran underpowered
(RQ3 above), not falsified. The segment-index arm does not reinstate that
falsification. An arbitrary ordinal carrying no provenance meaning reduces the
loss by $0.9476$ nats against true role labels' $0.8244$ nats, which is a
competing hypothesis about what the mechanism exploits, not a control task, and
not evidence that role semantics contribute nothing. Chapter~\ref{ch:results}
reports both comparisons and neither refutes the role-label claim. RQ3 is
answered negatively on the clinical detector's own three components, not on
this arm.

The controls were cheap. Logged control compute totals $1216.4$ GPU minutes across $79$ runs: the
AG News convergence sweep $555$ minutes over $48$ runs, a separate
single-budget comparison of the same four arms $34.9$ minutes over $12$ runs,
the clinical comparison $612.3$ minutes over $17$ runs, and the two signed
storage re-runs $0.7$ and $13.5$ minutes. Person hours were never logged, so
this is a compute only claim.

\section{Things to Check If You Are Building This}
\label{sec:conc-practice}

These are the checks that, had they been run when they were available, would
each have changed what was reported here.

Check that the evidence a candidate is displayed with is the evidence that
triggered it. The failure mode is the one drawn back in
Figure~\ref{fig:exemplar}: a rule that quantifies over a set of medication
mentions, an exemplar selector that indexes into that set positionally, and
nothing in between forcing the two to agree. The check is an assertion over two
data structures, and on the Rule 1 and Rule 2 gold stratum it is the check that
would have caught the largest upstream defect in this project before any
annotation was commissioned, a wrong rate as high as $0.4745$ (RQ1 above).

Estimate the base rate on a human labelled sample before generating weak labels,
and print the labeller's marginal beside it. A labeller positive on $261$ of
$299$ pairs against a base rate of $0.0401$ is diagnosable in one line when the
labels are produced, not after a detector has been trained on them, and the
dependence of agreement statistics on the marginals has been documented since
1990 \citep{feinstein1990paradoxes,byrt1993pabak}.

Report precision against the base rate of the pool the system ran on, with the
denominator stated, and prefer a coefficient that uses all four confusion matrix
cells when the classes are this skewed \citep{chicco2020mcc}. Precision of
$0.0424$ is a different object depending on
whether the pool's base rate is $0.005$ or $0.0474$, and here it is the latter.
At an operating point where the detector flags nearly the whole pool, precision
falling below base rate follows from the arithmetic of that operating point and
should be read beside kappa and the ranking metrics, not asserted alone.

Run a randomised counterpart of any component reported as learned, under the
same training conditions, and log the distance a learned parameter moved from
its initialisation. A parameter that finishes at $0.5076$ from a start of $0.5$,
agreeing to two decimals across seeds, is reporting its initialisation.

Run a positive control on the instrument before reporting a null from it, and
report achieved power alongside any comparison that returns no difference. The
positive control here, the pre-registered convergence sweep, cost $555$ minutes
over $48$ runs, and the strata comparison has $p = 0.7725$ at achieved power
$0.0576$, where the first number
without the second reads as evidence of no effect.

Keep the denominators separate and state them with every metric. The $299$
annotated pairs and the $253$ non-ambiguous pairs are different sets, and
conflating them in this project inverted a central claim that then survived
several rounds of internal review.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:conc-future}

Three pieces of work follow directly from the limitations, and a fourth
rejoins them where an earlier draft of this thesis reported it as already
settled. Each needs a resource this project did not have: the annotator's
time, an annotation budget, a controlled simulator, and, for the fourth, a
second model scale reachable through a channel this thesis can use
compliantly. That fourth item would order the over-calling multiplier by
parameter count rather than leaving it undetermined among the three local
models this project could run; the cell an earlier draft relied on to report
it came through a hosted channel this thesis has since withdrawn under the
compliance action Chapter~\ref{ch:method} records, so the question returns to
future work rather than staying answered.

\textbf{Re-annotate against corrected exemplars.} The base rate of $0.0401$
remains an upper bound rather than an estimate (RQ1 above). By reconstruction
the exemplar matched the trigger
for $72$ of the $137$ resolved Rule 1 and Rule 2 candidates, a rate of
$0.5255$; by an exact join covering far fewer rows, all $23$ of the $23$ it can
resolve match. A separate join across all four rules matches $35$ rows of the
annotated sample to the rebuilt candidates table
(Section~\ref{sec:res-rq1}), a different operation with a different
denominator, not a third reading of the same exemplar-correctness question. Rebuilding the
selector so the displayed pair is the pair that fired the rule, then
re-annotating the same candidate identifiers, would establish how much of the
measured base rate is real. The rules and the pool exist and the defect is
located, so this is bounded work.

\textbf{Assemble a cohort with more positives.} Every interval here rests on
$12$ positives. The minimum detectable AUROC at that count is $0.7355$ at
$80\%$ power and $\alpha = 0.05$, and the smallest stratum difference the
exemplar comparison could detect is $0.0895$ in absolute rate. A cohort large
enough to resolve effects where a useful clinical system would operate is the
single change that would most alter what can be concluded, and it means
annotation budget rather than modelling effort.

\textbf{Explain the selection channel anomaly.} Two selection channels on one
population with baseline disparity held fixed produce demographic parity drift
slopes of opposite sign, $-0.0078$ and $+0.0118$, and a controlled simulator
sweep inverts rather than reproduces the mapping. The pattern is stable and
reproducible and has no explanation. Five candidate mechanisms were proposed and
refuted inside this project; the sixth is left to work that can hold more of the
generating process fixed than this design allows.



\section{Closing}
\label{sec:conc-closing}

Dangerous medication contradictions between nursing notes and discharge summaries are real,
recurring, and worth detecting. A standard-shaped pipeline built for them does not detect them,
and the sharpest thing this thesis can say is that sentence together with the mechanism behind
it. The weak labeller assigns the contradiction class to the large majority of a pool whose
confirmed rate is a twentieth of that, and at that marginal the largest agreement any
thresholded label could express with the pharmacist is bounded before training begins. That
bound follows from two marginals. It needs no join, no significance test, no family count and
no vendor, and it is why the detector inherits a marginal rather than a judgement. Every other
failure in this thesis is attached to a mechanism of the same kind, read out of the code or the
counts rather than inferred from a null.

A comparison beside that result is worth reporting because of what it is not,
not because of what it shows. Every hosted-channel result this thesis
produced by transmitting these clinical sentence pairs to a third-party LLM
service is withdrawn under a compliance action Chapter~\ref{ch:method}
records, so the cross-model comparison an earlier draft of this thesis built
on that channel no longer exists in any form and is not reconstructed here.
What is left is entirely local. A separately prompted Llama 3 8B baseline, run
at the same eight-billion-parameter scale as the weak labeller and evaluated
on the same $253$ non-ambiguous pairs, returns a Matthews correlation whose
interval contains chance, the same verdict this pipeline's own detector
returns. A TF-IDF logistic regression on the $299$ annotated pairs ranks
above both, which is either surface-form signal or small-sample noise and is
not established either way (Chapter~\ref{ch:results}). None of the three
comparators this thesis can still report separates the confirmed cases from
the rest, so it has no demonstration, at any scale reachable through a
compliant channel, that a system built differently would have succeeded
where this pipeline failed.

What that leaves open is larger than what an earlier draft of this thesis
claimed. Whether the detector's chance-level ranking is a property of the
task as posed, of this pipeline's construction, or of the training signal it
was given is not settled by any surviving comparison, because the local
comparators available at a matched scale did not separate the confirmed
cases either. What is settled, and does not depend on that comparison, is the
mechanism: the weak labeller's marginal fixes an agreement ceiling before a
single pair is scored, four of the eight local labeller-and-basis rows this
pipeline measured could not have rejected the null at the conventional
threshold at any outcome, and the row that actually supplied the detector's
training signal is one of them. A detector trained on that signal was not
going to rank gold pairs above chance, and the finding this thesis can
defend is that the failure was diagnosable before training rather than only
after it.

The practical recommendation that follows is one sentence, and this project's
own record is the evidence for it rather than a success story. Before
training anything downstream of a weak labeller for a rare clinical event,
measure that labeller's positive rate against the gold standard's confirmed
rate and check what agreement its own marginal permits before spending a
training budget on the rest. This project's local evaluation baseline was
such a check run late rather than early: prompted once with no training, it
returned the same null on the same rows the finished pipeline did, at a
fraction of the engineering cost, which is the point the check makes even
when the baseline itself does not win. Run first, the same check would have
named the marginal ceiling this thesis instead spent a pipeline discovering.

Two of these findings should recur in direction in a differently built version of this
pipeline, and neither magnitude should be carried across: high-recall rules on an event this
rare produce a low-precision pool, and an instruction-tuned model asked to flag a rare class at
low prevalence over-flags it. What belongs to this build specifically will not necessarily
recur. The exemplar defect exists because this project's selector indexed positionally; the
architectural components report their initialisations because of how this project's parameters
were wired; and a second system shows a provenance-free position beating both true role
semantics and a randomised control from the same alphabet. The detector's chance-level ranking
is consistent with what the screening test named above already predicted before training, and
on its own it is not evidence about whether the failure belongs to the task or to this
construction, a question the withdrawal of this thesis's only cross-scale comparison leaves
open rather than closed.

None of that establishes that the task cannot be done, a limit stated once and not re-argued
here (Section~\ref{sec:disc-null}). What it establishes is narrower and more useful: each
failure has a located mechanism, and the failures a control could reach were separated from
measurement artifact by a control that fired. The harm head is the exception, and it is an
exception in the other direction: no control was needed, because no term in the objective ever
trained it.

A negative result that has been diagnosed is worth more than one that has not. Part of that
diagnosis was a set of controls that belong to other people's papers
\citep{lipsitch2010negative,adebayo2018sanity,hewitt2019control,egami2023surrogates,feinstein1990paradoxes},
cost $1216.4$ GPU minutes in total, and had not been run on this pipeline before this work;
running someone else's instrument is not inventing it. The rest of what this project leaves
behind is three checks of its own, named for what each does: trigger-exemplar dissociation, the
degeneracy precondition, and sign provenance. Each is cheap, each is a one-line assertion or a
pre-registered gate, and each catches a defect no reported metric here was sensitive to.
Neither half is a new method, and this thesis does not claim one; both are small, transferable
checks, offered so the next attempt starts further along than this one did.
