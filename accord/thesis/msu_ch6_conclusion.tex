\chapter{Summary and Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{What Was Asked}
\label{sec:conc-asked}

A nursing note records that a loop diuretic was held because the patient had
become hypovolemic; the discharge summary written five days later instructs
that the same drug be continued at the admission dose. Only one of those
documents leaves the hospital with the patient, and it is not the one
containing the reason the instruction is wrong.

This thesis asked whether that disagreement can be detected automatically
from paired clinical notes in MIMIC-III \citep{johnson2016mimic}, and built a
standard-shaped pipeline to answer it: rule-based candidate mining, language
model weak supervision, and a purpose-built neural detector over a
domain-pretrained clinical encoder. Each stage's reading was checked, and Section~\ref{sec:conc-found} states what survived that testing.

\section{What Was Found}
\label{sec:conc-found}

With 12 pharmacist-confirmed contradictions in 299 pairs, this evaluation
resolves only large AUROC effects: 80 percent power sits at 0.7466 for a
two-sided Mann-Whitney test and 0.8084 for the registered decision rule
(Section~\ref{sec:res-rq4}). The deployed labeller called 261 of the 299
pairs contradictions against 12 confirmed, and how far each 8B labeller
over-called turned on the prompt (Section~\ref{sec:res-rq2}). Its measured
MCC on the exact join, 0.0269, is at chance and below the 0.0780 ceiling its
marginal fixes (Section~\ref{sec:res-rq2}).

The detector trained on those labels was not distinguishable from chance on
the 253 non-ambiguous pairs, its ensemble's bootstrap interval excluding an
AUROC above 0.7034 (Section~\ref{sec:res-rq4}). The Rules 3 and 4 stratum is
not subject to the documented Rule 1 and Rule 2 positional defect, though its
exemplar correctness in the gold set is assumed, not verified; on its 108
non-ambiguous pairs the ensemble reaches 0.6095 (95 percent bootstrap
interval 0.3029 to 0.9010), a comparison between rule groups that with 6
positives can neither show nor exclude a defect artifact. By average
precision, chosen after its value was known, a bag-of-words model and an
inference model never trained on the weak labels clear their permutation
nulls where the detector does not, with no paired test separating the three
(Section~\ref{sec:res-rq4}).

\section{What This Establishes, and What It Does Not}
\label{sec:conc-establishes}

The feasibility check applies the classical maximum of agreement under fixed
marginals and the minimum attainable significance of a discrete test
\citep{cohen1960kappa,tarone1990modified}, a check that can be run on a weak
labeller before training and was applied here after the fact: at 261 flagged
of 299 no outcome could have rejected the null, the smallest attainable
one-sided $p$ being 0.1894. The measured MCC, 0.0269, sits below the
0.0780 ceiling, as it must, and is at chance. It bounds thresholded labels, not a ranking, so it is a
precondition rather than an explanation of the detector's null. The
resolvable-effect floor rests on a power analysis, not an empirical
validation of the clinical instrument, and the Rules 3 and 4 stratum, with
six positives, cannot show that the null is free of the exemplar defect.

\section{Practical Lesson}
\label{sec:conc-practice}

The lesson, stated as a lesson rather than as a demonstrated safeguard, is to
check a weak labeller's marginal against a human-established base rate before
training anything on its output, and to report a simple baseline's own
significance test alongside a neural ranking result. The exemplar check
(Figure~\ref{fig:exemplar}) is cheap relative to the training budget; that
is a reason to run it earlier next time, not a claim that running it here
would have changed what this evaluation could resolve at 12 positives.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:conc-future}

\textbf{A larger annotated set.} Every interval in this thesis rests on 12
positives, and the per-test floors at that count, 0.7466 and 0.8084, set the
size of the annotation effort a future attempt needs before a modest
detector could be told apart from chance.

\textbf{An independent set for wording-specific signals.} The strongest
pre-registered prompt cell, a reversed-direction llama3:8b score, did not
confirm under three new wordings (C109, Outcome B), and its signal sits in
the rows carrying the exemplar defect; granite3.3:8b's descriptive C109 cells
rank above chance under every wording, so whether a graded score escapes the
agreement ceiling is not settled (Section~\ref{sec:res-rq2}). A
wording-specific signal can only be tested on pairs it was not found on.

\textbf{A validated exemplar instrument.} Three instruments give $0$,
$0.4745$ and $0.9315$ for how often the exemplar selector showed a pair that
did not carry the conflict it asserted (Section~\ref{sec:r-exemplar}); the
highest comes from an extractor never validated against human annotation.
The bug pattern is reachable here, and its rate cannot be recovered after the
fact because the schema does not record the trigger; validating an instrument
would bound that rate and whether the pool's confirmed base rate is valid.

\textbf{Frontier-scale local labellers, when feasible.} The frontier-scale
comparison this project needed was attempted through a hosted API and
withdrawn under the compliance action Section~\ref{sec:m-ethics} records.
Whether over-calling attenuates with scale is answerable only by a labeller
served locally.

\section{Closing}
\label{sec:conc-closing}

Dangerous medication contradictions between nursing notes and discharge
summaries are real, recurring, and worth detecting. At the size this thesis
could annotate, twelve confirmed contradictions in 299 pairs, the pipeline
built to detect them did not, and the thesis leaves three results, the last two needing
no statistical power: a stage-wise audit, in which no stage's reported reading
survived its check: one not established, two unsupported, one a bounded null;
a feasibility check on the weak labeller's marginal, applied here after the
fact; and trigger-exemplar dissociation, a reachable mining bug pattern that
would be caught by a one-line assertion once the candidate schema records
which mention fired. In support, the detector shows no AUROC effect above
about 0.75 to 0.81. The planted-defect
benchmark did not reach its required firing rate, a limitation this thesis
discloses rather than a result it claims.

None of that establishes that the task cannot be done
(Section~\ref{sec:disc-null}). It establishes what a per-test floor of 0.7466
to 0.8084 lets a 253-pair evaluation say, and it leaves the size and the
design of the next attempt to the future work above.
