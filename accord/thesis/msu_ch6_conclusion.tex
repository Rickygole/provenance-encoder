\chapter{Summary and Conclusion}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{What Was Asked}
\label{sec:conc-asked}

A nursing note records that a loop diuretic was held because the patient had
become hypovolemic. The discharge summary written five days later instructs that
the same drug be continued at the admission dose. Only one of those documents
leaves the hospital with the patient, and it is not the one containing the
reason the instruction is wrong.

This thesis asked whether that disagreement can be detected automatically from
paired clinical notes in MIMIC-III \citep{johnson2016mimic}, and built the
pipeline the field would naturally build to
answer it: rule based candidate mining, language model weak supervision, and a
purpose built neural detector over a domain pretrained clinical encoder. Five
questions were posed, four about the stages and one about the field that
publishes pipelines like this one. Every stage failed a control.

\section{What Was Found}
\label{sec:conc-found}

\textbf{RQ1, candidate generation.} Four hand written rules enrich the candidate
space to $12$ confirmed contradictions in $299$ pharmacist annotated pairs, a
base rate of $0.0401$. That is a real enrichment over an unfiltered background
rate orders of magnitude lower, and it is nowhere near usable precision. The
finding that matters more than the rate is the exemplar defect: the mechanism
choosing which sentence pair to display was independent of the mechanism
deciding the candidate was a conflict, and across the full candidate pool the
displayed exemplar matched the trigger for only $0.586$ of candidates. The
annotator, the weak labeller and the model all reasoned about that evidence,
which makes $0.0401$ an upper bound on annotation validity rather than a clean
estimate of pool precision. The defect rate cannot be recovered on the annotated
sample itself: rebuilding the candidate table matched only $35$ of the $299$
audited rows, a match rate of $0.1171$, and of the $165$ rows from the two rules
capable of exhibiting the defect, $142$ are unresolved and the $23$ that resolve
are all correct.

Comparing exposed and unexposed strata does not settle it either. Rules 1 and 2
account for $165$ pairs with $6$ positives, a rate of $0.0364$; rules 3 and 4
account for $134$ pairs with $6$ positives, a rate of $0.0448$. Fisher exact
gives an odds ratio of $0.805$ at $p = 0.7725$, achieved power at that observed
effect is $0.0576$, and the smallest absolute rate difference detectable at
$80\%$ power is $0.0895$, more than twice the overall base rate. The strata are
indistinguishable at this sample size, which is not the same as equal.

\textbf{RQ2, weak supervision.} The weak labeller assigned the contradiction
class to $261$ of the $299$ annotated pairs, against $12$ true positives in the
same set. The detector distilled from those labels predicts contradiction for
$278$ of the $299$. A labeller positive on the large majority of a pool whose
true positive rate is $0.0401$ carries almost no information about which pairs
are contradictions, and the student inherited the marginal rather than the
judgment. Neither of the two standard corrections was available: the adjusted
count that would fix a biased prevalence estimate needs the labeller's error
rates on the same distribution \citep{forman2008quantifying}, and the
doubly robust correction for surrogate label bias needs a gold-labelled
subsample \citep{egami2023surrogates}, which is the resource the workflow
existed to avoid spending. The failure has a floor: the strongest labeller tested, on the $253$
non ambiguous pairs, flagged $71$ zero shot and was right about $6$, and flagged
$141$ few shot and was right about $7$. Better prompting moves the multiplier
without removing it, and the magnitude belongs to this pool's base rate rather
than transferring.

\textbf{RQ3, architecture.} None of the proposed components contributes what it
was credited with, and the controls establish this positively rather than by
absence of evidence. The authority parameters reported as learned finish at
$0.5076 \pm 0.0006$ and $1.0038 \pm 0.0010$ across seeds, against initialisation
at exactly $0.5$ and $1.0$; a parameter agreeing to three decimals across
independent seeds, within one percent of where it started, was not discovered by
training. The harm head emits scores spanning $0.3977$ to $0.6096$, the range of
an untrained sigmoid, and its Spearman correlation with the pharmacist's danger
rating over the $299$ pairs is $-0.0001$ at $p = 0.9991$. That null measures an
untrained module and says nothing about whether clinical severity is
predictable. Randomized substitutes for the remaining components recover most of
what the real components are credited with, which is the condition under which
an ablation delta stops being evidence about a component. The substitution is
the randomisation sanity check \citep{adebayo2018sanity} and the control task
\citep{hewitt2019control}, applied without modification to a different kind of
component; neither design is claimed here.

\textbf{RQ4, evaluation.} Three configurations sit at chance. On the $253$ non
ambiguous pairs with $12$ positives, the decontaminated three seed ensemble
reaches AUROC $0.5145$ with Mann-Whitney $p = 0.867$, and the per seed mean is
$0.5211 \pm 0.0201$. On the $299$ annotated pairs with the same $12$ positives,
under subject disjoint splits, the three seed mean AUROC is $0.4967 \pm 0.0350$,
and a DeLong test between the seed ensemble and the single model, which is the
correlated comparison the two share their cases with
\citep{delong1988comparing}, gives $p = 0.930$. The score distributions overlap: positives average $0.4198$ and
negatives $0.4282$, with Mann-Whitney $p = 0.6418$, so the positives score
marginally lower. A TF-IDF logistic regression on the same $299$ pairs reaches
AUROC $0.5761$ and average precision $0.1571$, above both neural
configurations, and whether that is surface form signal or small sample noise is
not established.

At the operating threshold the same conclusion appears as a cost. On the $253$
pair core the decontaminated detector's precision is $0.0424 \pm 0.0023$ and its
recall $0.7778 \pm 0.0393$, against a pool base rate of $0.0474$. Precision
below base rate means a flagged pair is marginally \emph{less} likely to be a
true contradiction than a pair drawn at random from the pool the detector
filtered. In the first seed that is $225$ pairs flagged, $9$ correct, $216$
wrong, $25$ true negatives and $3$ missed. Cohen $\kappa$ across seeds is
$-0.0105$ and expected calibration error is $0.8015$.

\textbf{RQ5, practice.} On a $75$ paper corpus deliberately enriched toward work
that attends to selection, $4$ papers audit the selection step under the strict
reading and $14$ under the permissive one, across $225$ quoted fields verified
against their sources. A blind second coder on $19$ overlapping papers gives
$\kappa = 0.855$ at $0.9474$ agreement on the permissive field and
$\kappa = 0.730$ at $0.7895$ on null attribute handling. On the strict field
both coders returned the same value on all $19$ papers, so agreement is $1.0$
and $\kappa$ is undefined; it is reported as undefined, not as perfect. The
enrichment runs against the finding: a corpus over sampling careful work still
yields $4$ of $75$. The check being counted is not an unreasonable one to ask
for. Stage level auditing has been demonstrated for fairness in tabular
preprocessing pipelines \citep{biswas2021fairpreprocessing}, and the reason a
selection step needs auditing before its outputs are interpreted has been argued
directly \citep{pfohl2025disaggregated}.

\section{What the Controls Changed}
\label{sec:conc-controls}

Without controls, everything above reads as a small underpowered study that
found nothing. The controls convert the same results into measurements with a
stated resolution.

The positive control carries the most weight. The device is the one
observational epidemiology formalised decades ago
\citep{lipsitch2010negative}: run the analysis where the answer is known, and
read the result as a statement about the instrument. Run on AG News with the
identical training harness and an adjudication rule committed before any run,
the
pretraining benefit is $4.53$ accuracy points at $24$ epochs, with seed standard
deviations at or below $0.0029$ and non overlapping bands. The same instrument
on the clinical task returns $0.0031$, between $0.7693 \pm 0.0102$ and
$0.7662 \pm 0.0073$. Eight times the training budget bought the scratch arm
$0.41$ points, so the arms were converged rather than starved. An instrument
that resolves $4.53$ points and returns $0.0031$ is reporting a null, not
failing to look. The general proposition that a pretraining objective predicts
downstream utility poorly is not established here; it is established elsewhere
and at a scale this project cannot approach
\citep{lourie2025scaling,feng2026tessera}. The sweep is a control, not a
contribution.

The decontamination control changed the headline. Excluding the evaluation
identifiers at load time moved AUROC on the same $253$ pairs from $0.4595$ to an
ensemble $0.5145$, from one side of chance to the other and nowhere useful in
either direction. The value of the control is not that change. It is that the
number reported before it was contaminated, and nothing in the metrics said so.

The sign provenance control changed no value and several conclusions.
Retrofitting signed storage for quantities persisted through an absolute value
reproduced every prior figure exactly, maximum discrepancy $0.0$, while
reversing seven of the fourteen comparable drift slopes. The demographic parity
slope is $-0.0078$ signed under one selection regime and $+0.0118$ under the
other, and both invert when stored as magnitudes. A quantity stored as a
magnitude licenses no directional claim.

The randomization controls changed the sign of the architecture argument. An
ablation that removes a component and observes a drop is consistent with the
component working and equally consistent with it absorbing capacity. A
randomized counterpart performing comparably is not consistent with it working.
Chapter~\ref{ch:results} reports the latter, which is why RQ3 is answered
negatively rather than left open.

The controls were cheap. Logged control compute totals $661.4$ GPU minutes: AG
News $34.9$ minutes over $12$ runs, the clinical comparison $612.3$ minutes over
$17$ runs, and the two signed storage re-runs $0.7$ and $13.5$ minutes. Person
hours were never logged, so this is a compute only claim.

\section{Things to Check If You Are Building This}
\label{sec:conc-practice}

None of what follows is a prescription; this thesis is in a weak position to
issue any. These are the checks that, had they been run when they were
available, would each have changed what was reported here.

Check that the evidence a candidate is displayed with is the evidence that
triggered it. Figure~\ref{fig:exemplar} shows the failure mode in the abstract:
a rule that quantifies over a set of medication mentions, an exemplar selector
that indexes into that set positionally, and nothing in between forcing the two
to agree. The check is an assertion over two data structures, and at $0.586$
agreement across the pool it is the check that would have caught the largest
upstream defect in this project before any annotation was commissioned.

\begin{figure}[H]
\caption{The check that was not run, drawn as a schematic. The rule fires on a
conflict between two sets of medication mentions; the exemplar shown to the
annotator, the weak labeller and the model is chosen by position instead. Slots
are abstract and no clinical text appears.}
\label{fig:exemplar}
\includegraphics[width=0.9\linewidth]{figures/fig-exemplar.pdf}
\end{figure}

Estimate the base rate on a human labelled sample before generating weak labels,
and print the labeller's marginal beside it. A labeller positive on $261$ of
$299$ pairs against a base rate of $0.0401$ is diagnosable in one line when the
labels are produced, not after a detector has been trained on them, and the
dependence of agreement statistics on the marginals has been documented since
1990 \citep{feinstein1990paradoxes,byrt1993pabak}.

Report precision against the base rate of the pool the system ran on, with the
denominator stated, and prefer a coefficient that uses all four confusion matrix
cells when the classes are this skewed \citep{chicco2020mcc}. Precision of
$0.0424$ is a different object depending on
whether the pool's base rate is $0.005$ or $0.0474$, and here it is the latter,
which makes the flag worse than no flag.

Run a randomized counterpart of any component reported as learned, under the
same training conditions, and log the distance a learned parameter moved from
its initialisation. A parameter that finishes at $0.5076$ from a start of $0.5$,
agreeing to three decimals across seeds, is reporting its initialisation.

Run a positive control on the instrument before reporting a null from it, and
report achieved power alongside any comparison that returns no difference. The
positive control here cost $34.9$ minutes over $12$ runs, and the strata
comparison has $p = 0.7725$ at achieved power $0.0576$, where the first number
without the second reads as evidence of no effect.

Keep the denominators separate and state them with every metric. The $299$
annotated pairs and the $253$ non ambiguous pairs are different sets, and
conflating them in this project inverted a central claim that then survived
several rounds of internal review.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:conc-future}

Three pieces of work follow directly from the limitations, and they are named as
work rather than as hedges.

\textbf{Re-annotate against corrected exemplars.} The base rate of $0.0401$ is
an upper bound on annotation validity because the exemplar matched the trigger
for only $0.586$ of the pool, and the join that would measure the defect on the
annotated sample recovers only $35$ of $299$ rows. Rebuilding the selector so
the displayed pair is the pair that fired the rule, then re-annotating the same
candidate identifiers, would establish how much of the measured base rate is
real. The rules and the pool exist and the defect is located, so this is bounded
work.

\textbf{Assemble a cohort with more positives.} Every interval here rests on
$12$ positives. The minimum detectable AUROC at that count is $0.7382$ at
$80\%$ power and $\alpha = 0.05$, and the smallest stratum difference the
exemplar comparison could detect is $0.0895$ in absolute rate. A cohort large
enough to resolve effects where a useful clinical system would operate is the
single change that would most alter what can be concluded, and it means
annotation budget rather than modelling effort.

\textbf{Explain the selection channel anomaly.} Two selection channels on one
population with baseline disparity held fixed produce demographic parity drift
slopes of opposite sign, $-0.0078$ and $+0.0118$, and a controlled simulator
sweep inverts rather than reproduces the mapping. The pattern is stable and
reproducible and has no explanation. Five candidate mechanisms were proposed and
refuted inside this project; the sixth is left to work that can hold more of the
generating process fixed than this design allows.

\section{Closing}
\label{sec:conc-closing}

Dangerous medication contradictions between nursing notes and discharge
summaries are real, recurring, and worth detecting. The pipeline the field would
naturally build for them does not detect them. Rule based mining produces a pool
at $0.0401$ with the exemplar matching the trigger for $0.586$ of candidates,
weak supervision produces a labeller positive on $261$ of $299$ pairs against
$12$ true positives, the architectural components report their initialisations
and lose to their own randomized substitutes, and the detector sits at chance on
both evaluations while operating at precision $0.0424$ below a base rate of
$0.0474$.

None of that establishes that the task cannot be done. At $12$ positives the
design cannot see an effect below AUROC $0.7382$, and the thesis says so in its
introduction as well as here. What it establishes is narrower and more useful:
each failure has a located mechanism, and each was separated from measurement
artifact by a control that fired.

A negative result that has been diagnosed is worth more than one that has not.
The difference between the two, in this project, was a set of controls that
belong to other people's papers
\citep{lipsitch2010negative,adebayo2018sanity,hewitt2019control,egami2023surrogates,feinstein1990paradoxes},
cost $661.4$ GPU minutes in total, and had not
been run on this pipeline before this work. That is the contribution, and it is
offered so the next attempt starts further along than this one did.
