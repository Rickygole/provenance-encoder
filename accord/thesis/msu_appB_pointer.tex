\section{Reference Tables Released with the Code}
\label{app:provenance}

Two reference tables belong to the record behind this thesis and are generated by the released analysis code: one process assembles the record and another checks the chapters' claims against it. Both are released with that code, not bound, because neither is read sequentially. The first is the record of reported quantities: 9,577 entries, each extracted automatically from a stored result file and listed with the file it was read from; 8,478 of those entries are citable, and the rest are superseded or withdrawn values kept so a reader can see what a corrected number replaced. The second is the claim and evidence trace, which scans the chapters for 1,390 numeric quantities and resolves 1,390 of them against the record, at the precision printed: of the 694 distinct registered quantities the trace names, 141 are matched uniquely and 553 are matched among more than one candidate and marked as indicative. The trace lists 0 quantities as untraced and omits none. A further 9 figures in Chapter 2 are quoted from the studies that chapter cites. This thesis generated none of them, and the trace counts them apart without matching them against the record.
