\section{Reference Tables Released with the Code}
\label{app:provenance}

Two reference tables belong to the record behind this thesis, generated from the analysis code released with this thesis: one process assembles this appendix and another checks the chapters' claims against it. Both are released with that code, not bound, because together they run to more than 30 pages of table, none of which is read sequentially. The first is the record of reported quantities: 6,328 entries, each extracted automatically from a stored result file and listed with the file it was read from; 5,239 of those entries are citable, and the rest are superseded or withdrawn values kept so a reader can see what a corrected number replaced. The second is the claim and evidence trace, which scans the chapters for 1,319 numeric quantities and resolves 1,316 of them against the record, at the precision printed: of the 654 distinct registered quantities the trace names, 198 are matched uniquely and 456 are matched among more than one candidate and marked as indicative. The trace lists 3 quantities as untraced and omits none. All of them are occurrences of the lower and upper Wilson interval endpoints of one pooled outside-trigger-set rate, 38.8 and 44.1 percent, which Chapter 1 and Chapter 4 each print in full: both endpoints go untraced in Chapter 1 and the lower endpoint in Chapter 4, because those sentences place the word percent before the digits and the check recognises the word only when it follows them. A further 9 figures in Chapter 2 are quoted from the studies that chapter cites. This thesis generated none of them, and the trace counts them apart without matching them against the record.
