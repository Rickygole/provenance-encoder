\section{Reference Tables Released with the Code}
\label{app:provenance}

Two reference tables belong to the record behind this thesis, generated from the analysis code released with this thesis: one process assembles this appendix and another checks the chapters' claims against it. Both are released with that code rather than bound, because together they run to more than thirty pages of table, none of which is read sequentially. The first is the record of reported quantities: 5,694 entries, each extracted from a stored result file automatically rather than transcribed by hand, listed with the file it was read from; 4,800 of those entries are citable, and the rest are superseded or withdrawn values kept so a reader can see what a corrected number replaced. The second is the claim and evidence trace, which scans the chapters for 1,165 numeric quantities and resolves 1,159 of them against the record, at the precision printed: of the 602 distinct registered quantities the trace names, 178 are matched uniquely and 424 are matched among more than one candidate and marked as indicative rather than definitive. The trace leaves 6 quantities untraced rather than omitted. Three are the lower and upper Wilson interval endpoints of one pooled outside-trigger-set rate, printed as 38.8 and 44.1 percent once each in Chapter 1 and once more (the lower endpoint only) in Chapter 4; those sentences place the word percent before the digits rather than after them, and the check recognises the word percent only when it follows the digits. The other three are the patient count and two performance figures quoted in Chapter 2 from an external published study, 271 patients, 71.5 percent and 68.2 percent, none of which this thesis generated.
