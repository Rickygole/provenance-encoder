\chapter{The Correction Record}
\label{app:corrections}

This work issued 72 numbered corrections and 9 post-freeze revisions. Each withdrew, narrowed or confirmed a claim against evidence, and each is reproduced in full, with its date and the claim it changed, in the supplementary file that accompanies this document. They are summarised rather than reprinted here because the complete record runs to twenty pages of table that no reader reads in sequence, and because the same treatment is already given to the register, the claim trace and the survey coding table in Section~\ref{app:provenance}.

Ten of them changed a number this thesis prints, and those ten are the ones a reader is most likely to want to follow: they are named by identifier in the supplementary record and cross-referenced from the chapters that carry their corrected values. The most consequential is the last. C63 established that the replication behind Chapter~\ref{ch:results}'s weak-supervision finding varied domain and model family while holding capability fixed, so the cell that would have decided whether over-calling magnitude belongs to the pool or to the labeller was never built. Filling it withdrew four claims, moved this thesis's answer to its fourth research question, and produced Section~\ref{sec:r-frontier}.

The record is kept because a thesis about controls that reported no failures of its own would be evidence of a project that had stopped checking, not of one that had nothing to find.
