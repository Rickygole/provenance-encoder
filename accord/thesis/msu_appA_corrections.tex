\chapter{The Correction Record}
\label{app:corrections}

This work issued 108 numbered corrections and 9 post-freeze revisions. Each withdrew, narrowed or confirmed a claim against evidence. They are summarised rather than reprinted here because the complete record runs to twenty pages of table that no reader reads in sequence, and the full text of every one of them is released with the analysis code rather than bound, which is the same treatment given to the register, the claim trace and the survey coding table. The earlier wording promised the complete record in a supplementary file that accompanied this document. That file is no longer bound, so the promise described a deposit that did not exist, which is the defect class this record is full of.

Ten of them changed a number this thesis prints, and those ten are the ones a reader is most likely to want to follow: they are named by identifier in the released record and cross-referenced from the chapters that carry their corrected values. The most consequential is C95 (of 108), the largest single withdrawal in the record, named here by identifier rather than by its position in the record because the record kept growing after it: C95 withdrew every result this work obtained by sending sentence pairs to a hosted model service, on the authority of PhysioNet's guidance of 24 September 2025, which requires that no third-party service retain the data and strongly recommends locally deployed models; under it the hosted routes are withdrawn, whatever an earlier post had listed. That removed the only variation in labeller capability the design contained, so the question of whether over-calling magnitude belongs to the pool or to the labeller is open again rather than answered, and this thesis says so rather than returning to the attribution an earlier correction had already retracted. The withdrawal is enforced by the numeric provenance gate rather than by this sentence: every withdrawn value is held under a namespace the gate excludes from the set a reported figure may resolve against, because the preceding withdrawal in this record was enforced by prose alone and would have resolved a withdrawn digit anywhere in the thesis without complaint.

The record is kept because a thesis about controls that reported no failures of its own would be evidence of a project that had stopped checking, not of one that had nothing to find.
