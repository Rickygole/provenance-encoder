\chapter{The Correction Record}
\label{app:corrections}

This work issued 109 distinct numbered corrections and 9 post-freeze revisions. Each withdrew, narrowed or confirmed a claim against evidence. They are summarised rather than reprinted here, because the complete record runs to twenty pages of table that no reader reads in sequence. The full text of every one of them is released with the analysis code rather than bound, the same treatment given to the record of reported quantities and the claim-and-evidence trace.

Several of them changed a number this thesis prints, and each is named by identifier in the released record and cross-referenced from the chapter that carries its corrected value. The most consequential is C95 (of 109), the largest single withdrawal in the record, named here by identifier rather than by its position because the record kept growing after it. C95 withdrew every result this work obtained by sending sentence pairs to a hosted model service, on the authority of PhysioNet's guidance of 24 September 2025, which requires that no third-party service retain the data and strongly recommends locally deployed models; under it the hosted routes are withdrawn, whatever an earlier draft had listed. That removed the only variation in labeller capability the design contained, so the question of whether over-calling magnitude belongs to the pool or to the labeller is open again rather than answered, and this thesis says so rather than returning to the attribution an earlier correction had already retracted. The provenance check enforces the withdrawal, not this sentence: every withdrawn value is kept apart in the record, in the portion the check excludes from the set a reported figure may resolve against. The preceding withdrawal in this record was enforced by prose alone, which would have let a reported figure resolve against a withdrawn digit anywhere in the thesis without complaint. The record is kept because a thesis about controls that reported no failures of its own would be evidence of a project that had stopped checking, not of one that had nothing to find.
