\chapter{The Correction Record}
\label{app:corrections}

This work issued 109 distinct numbered corrections and 9 post-freeze revisions. Each withdrew, narrowed, or confirmed a claim against evidence. Correction and check identifiers both begin with C, but the correction record's numbering is separate from the checks'. The corrections are summarised here, not reprinted, because the complete record runs to twenty pages of table no reader reads in sequence. The full text of each is released with the analysis code, the same treatment given to the record of reported quantities and the claim-and-evidence trace.

Several changed a number this thesis prints; each is named by identifier in the released record and cross-referenced from the chapter carrying its corrected value. The most consequential is C95 (of 109), the largest single withdrawal in the record, named by identifier, not position, because the record kept growing after it. C95 withdrew every result this work obtained by sending sentence pairs to a hosted model service, following PhysioNet's guidance of 24 September 2025. That guidance states that the data use agreement prohibits sharing access to the data with third parties, including sending it through APIs or using it on online platforms, and that third-party services must not store or retain it. It strongly recommends locally deployed models and permits a hosted service only where zero data retention, no training on the data, and no human review are verified; this project could not verify those conditions for the runs it had made, so the hosted routes are withdrawn, whatever an earlier draft had listed. That removed the only variation in labeller capability the design contained, so whether over-calling magnitude belongs to the pool or to the labeller is open again, and this thesis says so without returning to an attribution an earlier correction had retracted. The withdrawal is enforced by the provenance check, not by prose. Withdrawn values sit in a separate part of the record, and the check will not match a printed number against that part. The previous withdrawal was enforced by prose alone, so a printed number could have matched a withdrawn value unnoticed. The record is kept because a thesis about controls that reported no failures of its own would be evidence of a project that had stopped checking, not of one that had nothing to find.
